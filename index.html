Our Mission

Why We Created I-SABI

The world does not care about your ignorance. In fact, it leverages on it. The people who get ahead in life are not necessarily smarter than you. They have simply learned to use information as a weapon while the rest of us remain ignorant. That is why the rich keep getting richer and the poor stay poor. That is why certain cycles keep repeating.

Here is what most people miss: the one percent have access to information that the majority do not. They know how systems work. They understand the rules of the game. They have had conversations that opened their eyes to possibilities you have not even considered.

Coming from where we are, we are already at a disadvantage. Our parents did not teach us certain things. Our schools did not give us certain knowledge. Our environment did not expose us to certain conversations. Those are not accidents. Those are information gaps. And until you fill them, you will keep repeating the same patterns and making the same mistakes in carreer, relationships and major aspects of your growth journey.
